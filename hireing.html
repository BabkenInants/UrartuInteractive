Careers at Urartu Interactive

About Us

Urartu Interactive is an Armenian game development studio focused on creating innovative and memorable gaming experiences. Our mission is to develop unique games with engaging mechanics, strong atmosphere, and well-crafted storytelling. We combine creativity with modern technologies to deliver high-quality and immersive interactive experiences for players worldwide.
